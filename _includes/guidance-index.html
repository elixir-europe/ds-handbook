{%- comment -%} Filterable + searchable guidance index – the signpost counterpart of _includes/case-studies-index.html. Reuses its .cs-index layout styles. Two filter layers, built from the frontmatter of the guidance pages: • Topics – every `topics:` value used on at least one page. • Skills – every `skills:` value used on at least one page (tooltips from the ESCO subset in _data/guidance/skills.yml). Within a layer, chips are OR-ed (any selected topic); across layers they are AND-ed (matches a selected topic AND a selected skill).
